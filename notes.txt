let 
    // 1. The Anchor: Scan the fact table to find the earliest date
    MinDate = List.Min(Fact_Table[Transaction_Date]),

    // 2. The Horizon: Scan the fact table to find the absolute latest date
    MaxDate = List.Max(Fact_Table[Transaction_Date]),

    // 3. The Distance: Calculate how many days between the Anchor and Horizon
    DayCount = Duration.Days(MaxDate - MinDate) + 1,

    // 4. The Chain: Generate a continuos list of dates starting at the anchor
    DateList = List.Dates(MinDate, DayCount, #duration(1,0,0,0)),

    // 5. The Conversion: Turn the raw list of dates back to a standard table
    ConvertedToTable = Table.FromList(DateList, Splitter.SplitByNothing(), null, null, ExtraValues.Error),

    // 6. The Cleanup: Rename the column and lock in the data type
    RenamedColumns = Table.RenameColumns(ConvertedToTable, {{"Column1", "Date"}}),
    FinalTable = Table.TransformColumnTypes(RenamedColumns, {{"Date", type date}})

    FinalTable










Fact Table: Contains facts about transaction events
	Transaction Date: The date when the sale occurred. Randomly selected from the year 2023.
	Region Index Key: This key links the fact table to the Region table
	Product Index Key: This key links the fact table to the Product table
	Customer Type: Returning or New
	Sales Channel: Retail or Online
	Payment Method: Credit Card, Cash, Cash on Delivery or Transfer 
	Quantity Sold: Quantity of unit item sold
	Unit Cost: Cost of procurement on unit item sold
	Unit Price: Unit price of product item sold. This is the addition of Unit Cost and assinged margin. 
	Discount: Amount deducted to make a sale
	Total Revenue: Total sales made, discount percentage is accounted for.
	Standard Profit: Profit with discount not accounted for.
	Effective Profit: Profit with discount accounted for.
	Profitability Status: Net Loss or Net Profit depending on Effective Profit value.

Product Dimension: Atable to normalize the Product related columns
	Product Index Key: Unique Identifier for a product.
	Product Number: Unique identifier for a product in its category. Unique products in different categories can have similar Product Number.
	Product Category: Unique category for similar products.

Region Dimension: A table to normalize Region and Sales Rep, each sales rep handles an instances of all region. There are 5 sales rep and 4 Region so the instances in the table are a cross multiplication of all regions and sales rep. 
	Region Index Key: Unique Identifier for each instance in the table.
	Region: South, North, West, East.
	Sales Rep: Names of sales reps.

Calendar: Contains dates, Year, Quarter, Month and Days.
